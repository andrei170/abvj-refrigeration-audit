What Harris Does That You Could Do This Month
None of this requires a bigger budget than you already have
  • A page called "request service". One page whose only job is to take the job. You currently ask people to fill in a form marked "subject".
  • Financing on its own page. A compressor failure is an unplanned bill. The firm that shows a payment option gets the change-out; the firm that does not gets a repair.
  • A gallery of their own work. Proof that costs nothing but a phone camera.
  • Sixty-five years in business, said on every page. You have four years and say it once. Say it more.
  • A phone number you can tap. Theirs works on a phone. Yours does not.
The honest read

Harris is not out-spending you. They finished their website and you did not finish yours. That gap is a few days of work, not a budget.

Where You Can Win
Things that are true of you and hard for a sixty-five-year-old firm to copy
  • You have capacity right now. A crew of three doing ten jobs a week that could do twenty means you can promise speed and actually deliver it. An established firm at full stretch cannot.
  • You are in Franklin. Harris is in Morgan City. For St. Mary Parish customers nearer you, "we are local" is a stronger line from you than from them.
  • Residential and commercial both. Commercial refrigeration work in this parish is worth naming specifically rather than burying in a list of six.
  • Nobody nearby is measuring anything. We found no working tracking on any site we could read. The first firm that knows which calls came from where has an advantage none of the others can see.
What We Could Not Check
Marked honestly rather than guessed
  • Two local firms blocked our check. Southern AC and Heating and Doug's Service Company both returned a security block rather than a page, so we have not assessed either and they are not in the table.
  • Nobody's reviews, ratings, pricing, revenue, crew size or job volume, including yours.
  • Whether any of these firms advertise on Google, Meta or local radio. We read websites.
  • Tracking loaded after the page renders would not show up in a static read, so "none found" may understate what a rival has installed.
  • Every HVAC firm in St. Mary Parish. This is what we could find and verify in the time available, not a complete census.
