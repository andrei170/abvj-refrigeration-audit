It is a French energy-audit FAQ about heat pumps and energy savings, from whoever built the theme. It has nothing to do with air conditioning in St. Mary Parish, and it sits on the page where a customer goes to find out who you are. This is a five-minute fix and it is the first thing we would do.

Finding 3 — The Site Still Carries Somebody Else's Name
In the footer of every page: "© 2023 by AirCool. Proudly created with Wix.com" In the browser tab on your services page: "SERVICES | My Site" The phrase "My Site" appears 12 times across the three pages.
"AirCool" is the template. "My Site" is the factory default.

Your business is A B V J Refrigeration & Air Conditioning, trading since 2021 in Franklin. Your website currently credits a company called AirCool and calls itself "My Site". Google reads that tab title, and so does every person who opens a second tab to compare you against someone else.

Finding 4 — The Form Cannot Tell An Emergency From A Quote

A form with no phone field means you have to email somebody back and wait while they ring the next firm on the list. And because nothing asks whether the AC is out completely or they are pricing a new system for the spring, every enquiry lands looking identical and has to be sorted by hand.

Finding 5 — Nothing Is Measured, Which Is Why You Could Not Tell If The Agency Worked

This is the part that matters most, given what you have already spent

You have paid a lead generation company and a marketing agency, and you have said neither produced results. With no tracking installed, there was never a way to prove it either way — not how many people arrived, not what they did, not which ones called. Any agency could have told you anything. So could we.

We would not ask you to spend a dollar on advertising until the meter is on, because the first thing we would want is a number you can check yourself.

What We Would Do, In Order
  • Make the phone tappable and put the meter on. A tel: link on every page and GA4 plus call tracking, so within two weeks you can see arrivals and calls yourself. This costs nothing and is the first day's work.
  • Delete the placeholder text and the French FAQ, and write the six services properly — what breaks, what it costs to look at, how fast you can get there.
  • Replace the form with one that sorts. Phone, residential or commercial, what has failed, how urgent, which town — so an outage never sits in an inbox looking like a spring quote.
  • Put your own proof on it. Photographs of your own installs and reviews from your own customers, named.
  • Only then spend on getting in front of people, with a number you can check against the work booked.

You have a crew of three doing ten jobs a week who could do twenty.

The capacity is already paid for. Fix where the enquiries land before buying any more of them.
